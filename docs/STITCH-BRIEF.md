# Google Stitch Brief — Cognitive Ledger

**Status:** `NOT_READY / RESERVED`
**Date:** 2026-08-21

This document is intentionally reserved for a later phase.

The product team has explicitly chosen to mature the product concept, behavior, domain model, information requirements and user journeys **before** asking Google Stitch to explore UX/UI.

## Inputs required before this brief becomes actionable

- validated product vision;
- agreed domain model;
- capture and retrieval behavior;
- source-of-truth and privacy model;
- primary user journeys;
- MVP capability boundary;
- representative data/examples;
- states and edge cases;
- success criteria.

## What Stitch should eventually receive

The final brief should specify what the product must enable and what information must be visible, while leaving visual composition, navigation patterns, typography, color, component styling and interaction aesthetics open for Stitch exploration.

## Current non-goal

Do not prematurely lock UI details during architecture/product discovery.
